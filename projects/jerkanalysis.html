Motion / Jerk Analysis

Summary
Created a experimental design to analyze jerk behavior in tennis rackets during a swing. The goal was to determine methods for reducing jerk experienced by the player during competition.
Role
Team member, responsibilities split equally between two.
Challenges
Incoporating the accelerometers was the biggest challenge we expereinced. We ran into multiple complications convertign the acclerometer date into usable data, troubleshooting accelerometer placement and soldering connections, and creating an intuitive data streaming pipeline. Once these were set up, using a preplanned DOE made the exeperiment much easier to run and analyze.
Results
The analysis revealed significant jerk peaks during impact scenarios. The ending recommendation matched a lot of common wisdom on how to grip and hold a racket during swinging, which validated our results.
