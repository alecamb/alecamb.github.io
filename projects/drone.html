Drone Project

Summary
Designed and tested an assembly for a racing drone
Role
Team member primarily focused on propeller design, CFD, and FEA analysis to determine feasability of design
Challenges
There were several challenges in this project. First, creating the propellers required unique design planning to create the smooth shape required to push air efficiently. Second, the CFD analysis were complicated because of how the drone moves and interacts with the surrounding air. We had to account for liftoff situations along with actual in flight scenarios.
Results
The custom propeller design successfully produced force capable of lifting the drone, as seen below. Furthermore, each component of the assembly was designed based off of real specifications. Because of this, we could prove that our custom assembly design was feasbile to build and would work in real life.
