LVAD Project

Summary
Designed a wireless Left Ventricular Assist Device (LVAD) concept for improved patient mobility and quality of life.
Role
Mechanical Engineer. Led fluid pump integration, architected cardiovascular validation system, managed project constraints
Challenges
Overcame stringent FDA regulations on low power transmissions by adapting a high-efficiency fluid pump capable of meeting flow requirements on minimal wattage. Designed and fabricated modular mock circulatroy loop using a specialized water-polyme fluid to precisely replicate human blood viscosity and density. Engineered the system to accurately mimic the fluid states at the inlet of the aorta, iteravely troubleshooting fluid containment at operational pressures.
Results
Successfully created experimental apparatus that met cardiovascular flow rates of 6 L/min against a back pressure of 2.1 psi throughout a 1 in diameter system with only a 6 Watt input.
